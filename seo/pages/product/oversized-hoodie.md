## Do heavyweight hoodies shrink?

OTRACE heavyweight hoodies are pre-shrunk 450 GSM cotton fleece. Wash them cold and tumble dry low. High heat is what shrinks cotton hoodies.

## What is 450 GSM hoodie?

A 450 GSM hoodie is cotton fleece weighing 450 grams per square metre. The OTRACE Oversized Hoodie is that weight: heavyweight loopback fleece, brushed on the inside, garment dyed.

## Are hoodies unisex?

Yes. OTRACE hoodies are unisex, sized XS to 3XL. Take your usual size for the oversized fit, or size down for a cleaner line.

## Boxy hoodie meaning

A boxy hoodie is cut square through the body with a dropped shoulder, not tapered at the waist. The OTRACE Oversized Hoodie is cut that way.
