## What is your return policy?

Unworn items with tags attached can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund or an exchange. Email us and we send a label. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days of the parcel reaching us.

If a piece arrives faulty or is not what was ordered, tell OTRACE within 30 days and return shipping is covered.
