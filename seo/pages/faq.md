## Do heavyweight hoodies shrink?

OTRACE heavyweight hoodies are pre-shrunk 450 GSM cotton fleece. Wash them cold and tumble dry low. High heat is what shrinks cotton hoodies.

## What is 450 GSM hoodie?

A 450 GSM hoodie is cotton fleece weighing 450 grams per square metre. The OTRACE Oversized Hoodie is that weight: heavyweight loopback fleece, brushed on the inside, garment dyed.

## Are hoodies unisex?

Yes. OTRACE hoodies are unisex, sized XS to 3XL. Take your usual size for the oversized fit, or size down for a cleaner line.

## Boxy hoodie meaning

A boxy hoodie is cut square through the body with a dropped shoulder, not tapered at the waist. The OTRACE Oversized Hoodie is cut that way.

## Do sweatpants shrink in the dryer?

These sweatpants are pre-shrunk 450 GSM cotton fleece. Tumble dry low. High heat is what shrinks cotton sweatpants in the dryer.

## 450 GSM sweatpants

450 GSM sweatpants are heavyweight cotton fleece at 450 grams per square metre. The OTRACE Wide-Leg Sweatpant is that fleece, garment dyed, cut wide, with a ribbed ankle.

## What does garment dyed mean?

Garment dyed means the piece is sewn first and dyed afterwards, so the colour settles into the seams. OTRACE hoodies and sweatpants are garment dyed 450 GSM cotton fleece in Onyx Black and Warm Cream.

## Garment dyed vs piece dyed

Piece dyed means the fabric is dyed before it is cut and sewn. Garment dyed means the finished piece is dyed after sewing, so seams and body take the dye together. OTRACE is garment dyed, and small variation between pieces is normal.

## Does garment dye fade?

Garment dye softens with wear rather than fading out. OTRACE dyes the hoodie and sweatpant after sewing. Wash cold, inside out, and tumble dry low.

## Oversized hoodie size chart

Chest, body length and shoulder are garment-flat centimetres. XS chest is 40 cm, which is 15.7 inches. Take your usual size for the oversized fit, or size down for a cleaner line.

## Wide leg sweatpants size chart

Outseam, inseam and leg opening are garment-flat centimetres. The waist is true to size and the leg is cut wide with a ribbed ankle. XS outseam is 76 cm, or 29.9 inches.

## How should an oversized hoodie fit?

An oversized hoodie should sit roomy through the body, with a dropped shoulder, longer than a standard fit. Take your usual size for that look, or size down for a cleaner line. This hoodie is cut a full size large on purpose.

## Unisex hoodies size chart

OTRACE hoodies are unisex, XS to 3XL. This size chart is the same garment-flat measurement for every body. Take your usual size for the oversized fit.
