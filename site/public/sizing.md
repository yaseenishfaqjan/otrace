# Sizing

HTML: https://otrace.co/sizing

Measurements are garment-flat centimetres. Inches are centimetres divided by 2.54, rounded to one decimal. Sizes run XS, S, M, L, XL, 2XL, 3XL. The chart is unisex.

## Oversized hoodie size chart

Chest, body length and shoulder are garment-flat centimetres. XS chest is 40 cm, which is 15.7 inches. Take your usual size for the oversized fit, or size down for a cleaner line.

## Wide leg sweatpants size chart

Outseam, inseam and leg opening are garment-flat centimetres. The waist is true to size and the leg is cut wide with a ribbed ankle. XS outseam is 76 cm, or 29.9 inches.

## How should an oversized hoodie fit?

An oversized hoodie should sit roomy through the body, with a dropped shoulder, longer than a standard fit. Take your usual size for that look, or size down for a cleaner line. This hoodie is cut a full size large on purpose.
