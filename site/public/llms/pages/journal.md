Three notes on the AW2026 fleece: what 450 GSM means, how garment dyeing differs from piece dyeing, and how the oversized hoodie is meant to fit.

- [450 GSM hoodie meaning](https://otrace.co/blog/what-is-a-450-gsm-hoodie.md): 450 GSM means the fleece weighs 450 grams per square metre. The OTRACE Oversized Hoodie is that weight: heavyweight cotton, brushed inside, garment dyed.
- [Garment dyed vs piece dyed](https://otrace.co/blog/garment-dyed-vs-piece-dyed.md): Piece dyed means the fabric is dyed before it is cut and sewn. Garment dyed means the finished hoodie is dyed after sewing, so the seams take the colour too.
- [Oversized hoodie fit](https://otrace.co/blog/how-an-oversized-hoodie-should-fit.md): An oversized hoodie should sit roomy, with a dropped shoulder. The OTRACE hoodie is cut a full size large. Take your usual size, or size down for a cleaner line.
